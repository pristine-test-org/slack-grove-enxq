How Cowslip works

One calm page that shows the shared list the way your team left it, without logins or noise.

Cowslip reads the shared list each time the page opens, so what you see is what everyone else sees. Nothing is stored in your browser, and there is nothing to set up before you start.

  1. Open the front door and read the heading.
  2. Scroll to the live list to see the newest five items.
  3. Items with a tick are finished; the rest are still open.
